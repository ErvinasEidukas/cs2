CS2 Economy

Loss Bonus

The losing team receives increasing money after consecutive losses. Winning a round resets the loss bonus.

Consecutive Losses Reward
1st loss $1,400
2nd loss $1,900
3rd loss $2,400
4th loss $2,900
5+ losses $3,400

T lose after planting (CT defuse) Every Terrorist receives an additional $800 compensation + the current loss bonus.
