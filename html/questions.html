Questions

What should I do in a force-buy round?

Description - A force buy usually happens after losing the pistol round, when your team does not have enough money for a full buy and will not be able to afford a full buy after an eco round.

Golden rule - Do what the majority of your team is doing. If they are forcing, force; if they are ecoing, go eco.

Example - If your team is force-buying, it is usually best to buy a Five-SeveN or Tec-9 with armor and play together.

Rule - Only save if you are specifically building money for an AWP.

Tip - On FACEIT, when playing solo, it is often better to follow what the majority of your team is doing. Sometimes it is worth waiting for a teammate to die, picking up their weapon, and continuing the round with it.

Note - The majority of force buys will be lost, but it is still better than two eco rounds or one eco + half buy.

What should I buy during the pistol round?

Rule - Always spend all of your money.

Rule - Avoid buying more than two grenade sets for the team.

Rule - The player with Dual Berettas must have armor.

How should I buy during a full-buy round?

Rule - Always buy as much utility as possible.

Rule - Do not buy more than three defuse kits for the team.

Rule - Buy a helmet only when necessary.

Buy priority - Helmet (if needed) → utility → defuse kit if money remains.

What should I do with an SMG if the enemy has a full buy?

Golden rule - Keep using the SMG until you find a better weapon. Never buy a new gun to replace the SMG.

Example - Play as support, help your teammates with grenades, and be ready to trade them in bad duels. Alternatively, play as bait and let your teammates trade you.

How should I play the pistol round?

Description - Pistol rounds are mostly about individual aim and positioning.

Tip - If you are going first, try to pull the enemy's crosshair away by moving unpredictably or jumping out of a corner. Most likely, you will die, but your role is to give your teammates as much time as possible to trade you.

Tip - If you are going second, your job is to trade your teammate as quickly as possible.

What if nobody is making calls on the T side?

Reality - You will always encounter teammates who do not communicate, even at high FACEIT ratings.

Tip - Take the initiative, play your own game, and make calls for the plays you want to execute.

How should I play clutch situations?

Rule - Take only isolated 1v1 fights. Avoid giving multiple opponents the chance to peek you at the same time.

Goal - Your job is not win the round. You must try to deal as much damage to the enemy economy as possible.

When should I save?

Rule - If your team has an advantage (for example, a numbers advantage), you should always attempt the retake.

Rule - If your team does not have an advantage, look for mistakes from your opponents. Try to punish poor rotations, weak positions, predictable angles, or players that can be cleared with utility.

Rule - If you fail to create an advantage, saving your weapon is usually the best decision.

How much information should I give to my teammates?

Rule - Your teammates should always know what you see, what you hear, and what you are planning to do.

Example - Tell your teammates what you are watching, what you are doing, what you plan to do next, and when you need support.

Example - Call when the enemy AWPer is missing from common positions and share your expectations about enemy rotations if it helps the team.

What is the best key for voice chat?

Tip - Use whatever key feels comfortable.

Example - Many players prefer Z, X, or C because they are easy to reach.

Should I crouch during gunfights?

Golden rule - Against pistols, crouching is usually a mistake.

Note - Against rifles, crouching is not necessarily wrong, but once you commit to crouching, you will not be able to escape from the duel.
