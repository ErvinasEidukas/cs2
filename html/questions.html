Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do in a force buy round?

If your team is force buying, it is usually best to buy a Five-SeveN or Tec-9 with armor and play together.

Only save if you are specifically building money for an AWP.

On FACEIT, when playing solo, it is often better to follow what the majority of your team is doing. Sometimes it is worth waiting for a teammate to die, picking up their weapon, and continuing the round with it.

How should I buy during the pistol round?

Always spend all of your money.

Avoid buying more than two grenade sets for the team.

How should I buy during a full buy round?

Always buy all useful utility.

Do not buy more than three defuse kits for the team.

Buy a helmet only when necessary.

Buy priority: helmet (if needed) → utility → defuse kit if money remains.

Should I replace my SMG after won eco round?

Keep using the SMG until you find a better weapon. Never buy a new gun to replace the SMG.

How should I play the pistol round?

Pistol rounds are heavily based on movement and mechanics.

If you enter first, try to pull enemy crosshair placement by moving unpredictably with strafes and jumps.

If you enter second, your job is to trade your teammate as quickly as possible.

What if nobody is making calls on the T side?

You will always encounter teammates who do not communicate, even at high FACEIT ratings.

Take the initiative, play your own game, and make calls for the plays you want to execute.

How should I play clutch situations?

Try to isolate as many 1v1 fights as possible.

Avoid giving multiple opponents the chance to peek you at the same time.

When should I save?

If your team has an advantage (for example, a numbers advantage), you should always attempt the retake.

If your team does not have an advantage, look for mistakes from your opponents. Try to punish poor rotations, weak positions, predictable angles, or players that can be cleared with utility.

If you fail to create an advantage or find an opening, saving your weapon is usually the best decision.

How much information should I give my teammates?

Give short, clear, and constant information.

Tell your teammates what you are watching, what you are doing, what you plan to do next, and when you need support.

Call when the enemy AWPer is missing from common positions and share your expectations about enemy rotations if it helps the team.

What is the best key for voice chat?

Use whatever key feels comfortable.

Many players prefer Z, X, or C because they are easy to reach without affecting movement.

Should I crouch during gunfights?

Against pistols, crouching is usually a mistake because it can make your head easier to hit.

Against rifles, crouching is not necessarily wrong, but once you commit to crouching, the duel is usually decided very quickly. Use it intentionally rather than automatically.
