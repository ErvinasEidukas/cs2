Frequently Asked Questions

What is an eco round?

An eco round is a round where players spend little or no money to save for a stronger buy in a future round.

What is a force buy?

A force buy is when a team spends nearly all of its money despite not being able to afford a full loadout.

What is the loss bonus?

Teams receive increasing money after consecutive round losses, helping them recover economically.

Why are callouts important?

Callouts allow teammates to communicate enemy positions quickly and accurately.

What is utility?

Utility refers to grenades such as smoke grenades, flashbangs, HE grenades, incendiaries, and decoys.
